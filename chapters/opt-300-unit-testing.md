# OPT-3. Unit Testing

**Goal:** Write some unit tests for some of your application and your NGRX functionality. Understand why testing is important even though it's painful.

## Mandatory Materials

**Videos**

1. [Angular: Testing Standalone](https://youtu.be/Jv7jOrGTKd0)
2. [Software Testing Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u6QfIXgjwGQ)

**Reading**
- [Angular: Testing](https://angular.dev/guide/testing)
- [NGRX: Store Testing](https://ngrx.io/guide/store/testing)
- [NGRX: Effects Testing](https://ngrx.io/guide/effects/testing)
- [Angular: Testing with overrideComponent](https://angular.dev/guide/testing/components-scenarios#the-overridecomponent-method)

## Online Shop:

> Write tests for at least the following:
> - One of your services (preferably the one that handles user roles / authentication),
> - One of your container components (test the typescript logic, preferably choose a component with more complex logic),
> - One of your presentitonal component (do a [DOM test](https://angular.dev/guide/testing/components-basics), preferably choose a component with more complex template logic (if/else present))
> - One of your reducers.
> - One of your effects.
