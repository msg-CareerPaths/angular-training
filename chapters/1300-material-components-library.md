**Goal:** Learn to use a component library.


## Mandatory Materials

**Reading**

- [Material - Introduction](https://m2.material.io/design/introduction)

## Online Shop:

> Install and use [Material Design](https://material.angular.dev/guide/getting-started) in your Online Shop app. Replace some of your "plain" HTML components with [Material Components](https://material.angular.io/components/categories).
> For example, replace your plain table with [Material Table](https://material.angular.dev/components/table/overview) and your plain buttons with [Material Buttons](https://material.angular.io/components/button/overview)
